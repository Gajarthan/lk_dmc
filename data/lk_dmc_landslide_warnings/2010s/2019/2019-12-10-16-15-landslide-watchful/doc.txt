1. Pay your attention to the following pre landslide signs,
F
Development ofcracks on the ground, deepened cracks and ground subsidence
F
Slanting oftrees, electrical posts, fences and telephone posts
)
Cracks in the floors and walls ofbuildings which are built at slopes
F Sudden appearance of springs, emerging muddy water, blockage or disappearance of existing
springs
2. People should move immediately away from the areas where they noticed the above preJandslide signs.
3. Fudhermore, people living in landslide susceptible areas should be extra vigilant and should be ready to
move quickly to safe places ifheavy rain continues.
