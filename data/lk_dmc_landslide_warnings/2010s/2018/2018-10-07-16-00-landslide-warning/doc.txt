Special Note: During this Landslide Early Waming period, please pay attention to the following:
1. Pay your attention.to the following preJandslide signs,
) Development of cracks on the ground, deepened cracks and ground subsidence
F
Slanting oftrees, electrical posts, fences and telephone posts
F Cracks in the floors and walls of buildings which are built at slopes
F Sudden appearance of springs, emerging muddy water, blockage or disappearance of existing
springs
2. People should move immediately away from the areas where they noticed the above pre-landslide signs.
3. Furthermore, people living in landslide susceptible areas should be extra vigilant and should be ready to
move quickly to safe places ifheavy rain continues.
Note: Communities are advised to follow the instnrctions given by National Building Research Organization and
