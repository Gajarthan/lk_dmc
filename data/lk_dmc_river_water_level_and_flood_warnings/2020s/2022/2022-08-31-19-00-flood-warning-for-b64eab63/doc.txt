Kalu River as of 7.00 pm today. By analyzing the current rainfall situation and river water levels of
Hydrological Stations in Kuda Ganga, there is a possibility of occurring a minor flood situation in

through those areas. Disaster Management Authorities are requested to take adequate precautions
