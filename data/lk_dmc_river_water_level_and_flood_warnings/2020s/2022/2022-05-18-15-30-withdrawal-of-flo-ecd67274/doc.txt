Withdrawal of Flood Warning in Kalu Basin
Significant rainfall has not been reported from Kalu river basin during last 24 hours and the high
river water levels reported in Rathnapura & Kalawellawa (Millakanda) in that river basin have been
dropped down considerably. The risk of flooding in that river is now considerably receding. The
remaining flood waters in lowline areas in that river basin will also be gradually draw down within
next few days. Therefore, the Flood Warning issued for the Kalu Basin, by the Hydrology and
Disaster Management Division of the Irrigation Department on 14.05.2022 at 8.00 pm is hereby
withdrawn.
