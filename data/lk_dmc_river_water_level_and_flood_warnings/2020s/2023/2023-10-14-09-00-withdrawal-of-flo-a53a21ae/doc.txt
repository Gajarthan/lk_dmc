Withdrawal of Flood Warning in Gin River Basin
Significant rainfall has not been reported from Gin River basin during last 24 hours and the high river
water levels reported in Thawalama and Baddegama in that river basin have been dropped down
considerably. The risk of flooding in that river is now considerably receding. The flood waters, if
remaining still in lowline areas in that river basin, such water will also be gradually draw down soon.
Therefore, the Flood Warning issued for the Gin River, by the Hydrology and Disaster
Management Division of the Irrigation Department on 28.09.2023 at 9.00 am is hereby
withdrawn.
