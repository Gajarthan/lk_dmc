WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS
Issued at 12.00 noon on 05 July 2023
Condition of Rain:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Hambantota via Colombo and Galle. A few showers will occur in the other sea areas
around the island.
Winds:
Winds will be south-westerly and speed will be (30-40) kmph.
Wind speed may increase up to (60-70) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending
from Trincomalee to Pottuvil via Kankasanthurai, Mannar, Puttalam, Colombo, Galle and
Hambanthota.
State of Sea:
The sea areas off the coast extending from Trincomalee to Pottuvil via Kankasanthurai,
Mannar, Puttalam, Colombo, Galle and Hambanthota will be rough, very rough at times.
It is dangerous to engage in activities in the above sea areas. Therefore, Naval and fishing
communities are requested to be vigilant in this regard.
Other sea areas around the island will be fairly rough.
Increase of swell waves (about 2.5 – 3.0 m) can be expected in the sea areas off the coast
extending from Mannar to Pottuvil via Puttalam, Colombo, Galle and Hambanthota.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
