The warning issued by the flood warning message No. 04 issued for the Kuda Ganga sub basin of Kalu
River basin on 05.06.2023 at 04.30 PM, is extended for the next 24 hours. According to the rainfall
situation and river water levels of Hydrological Stations in Kuda Ganga, it is expected to continue the
minor flood situation in lowline areas of Kuda Ganga valley situated in Palinda Nuwara and
Bulathsinhala D/S Divisions. As a result, further inundation of minor roads running through flood plains
of Kuda Ganga is expected. It is requested to pay high attention in these regards by the residents in those
area and vehicle drivers running through those areas. Disaster Management Authorities are requested
to take adequate precautions in this regards.
