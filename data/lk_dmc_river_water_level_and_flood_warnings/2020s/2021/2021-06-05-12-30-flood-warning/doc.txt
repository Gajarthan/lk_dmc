high possibility of considerably increasing current Flood Situation in lowline areas of Kelani River Valley
situated in Dehiovita, Ruwanwella, Seethawaka, Dompe, Homagama, Kaduwela, Biyagama, Kolonnawa and
Wattala D/S Divisions. The residents of the area and drivers of the vehicles running through those areas are
requested to pay high attention in this regard in order to save valuable lives and public and private properties.
