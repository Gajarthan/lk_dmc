It is observed that a considerable rainfall has been occurred at Kuda Ganga Sub Basin in Kalu Ganga
Basin during last 24 hours. In addition, water level of the river at upstream has risen to a considerable
level. The water level at Millakanda River Gauging Station situated closed to Kalawellawa has risen
up to 7.25 m at 2:00 PM today. Owing to this situation, there is a possibility of occurring a minor
flood situation in lowline areas of Kalu River Basin in Bulathsinhala, Baduraliya, Parinda
Nuwara, Millaniya, Horana, Dodangoda and Kaluthara DS division areas within next few
hours. Therefore, public living in those areas and drivers running through roads in those
lowline areas are requested to be highly vigilant in this regards. Disaster Management Authorities
are requested to take suitable actions in this regards.
