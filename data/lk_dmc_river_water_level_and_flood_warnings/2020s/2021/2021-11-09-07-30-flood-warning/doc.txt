As per the warning issued by the Irrigation Department for Maha Oya River Basin at 10.00 pm
yesterday (2021.11.08), the river water levels at the upstream areas have been risen extensively. The
water level at Giriulla River Gauging Station has been risen up to 8.94 m at 7:00 AM today. It is
warned that there is a high possibility of occurring a Major Flood Situation in lowline areas of
Maha Oya Valley situated in Alawwa, Diwulapitiya, Meerigama, Pannala, Wennappuwa,
Negambo, Katana, Narammala and Dankotuwa D/S Divisions within next few hours. The
residents of the area and drivers of the vehicles running through those areas are requested to pay
high attention in these regards in order to save valuable lives and public and private properties. The
Disaster Management Authorities and District/ Divisional Administrations are requested to
take necessary precautions in these regards (Please see attached map and list of GN Divisions
under threat of flooding – Attachment 01).
