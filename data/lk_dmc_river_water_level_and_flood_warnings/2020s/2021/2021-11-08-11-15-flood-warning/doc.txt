today. Owing to this situation, there is a possibility of occurring a minor flood situation in lowline
areas of Attanagalu oya River Basin in Attanagalla, Gampaha and J-Ela DS division areas within
next few hours. Therefore public living in those areas and drivers running through roads in those
lowline areas are requested to be highly vigilant in this regards. Disaster Management Authorities
are requested to take suitable actions in this regards.
