Minor Flood Warning for the Mahaweli River Basin
Due to weather forecasts predicting further rainfall in the upper catchment areas of the Mahaweli River,
and the recent opening of the spillway gates of the Kotmale Reservoir, there is a risk of a minor flood
situation developing within the next 24 hours. This warning applies to the low-lying areas associated
with the Mahaweli River located within the following Divisional Secretariat Divisions: Udunuwara,
Udapalatha, Yatinuwara, Doluwa, Ganga Ihala Korale, Gangawata Korale and Harispattuwa. Residents
living in the low-lying areas adjacent to the Mahaweli River in these regions are strongly advised to
remain highly vigilant and take necessary precautions to safeguard themselves against a potential flood.
Disaster management authorities are requested to take the necessary proactive measures in this regard.
As further rainfall is anticipated, the general public is earnestly requested to exercise extreme caution
and remain strictly alert to the developing situation.
