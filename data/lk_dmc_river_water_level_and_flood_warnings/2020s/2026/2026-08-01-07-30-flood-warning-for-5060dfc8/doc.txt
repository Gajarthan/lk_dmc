Significant, high-intensity rainfall has currently been recorded in the upper and middle catchment areas
of the Kelani River basin. Based on these rainfall conditions and the analysis of water levels from river
gauges maintained by the Irrigation Department within the Kelani River basin, a warning is hereby
issued that a Minor Flood Situation may develop within the next 48 hours in the low-lying areas of
the Kelani River valley belonging to the Dehiowita, Ruwanwella, and Seethawaka Divisional
Secretariat Divisions. Furthermore, because current forecasts indicate that future high-intensity
rainfall could occur, potentially causing a sudden rise in river water levels, residents of these areas,
river users, and motorists traveling through the region are strongly urged to act with extreme
caution . Disaster management authorities are also requested to take all necessary measures
regarding this situation.
