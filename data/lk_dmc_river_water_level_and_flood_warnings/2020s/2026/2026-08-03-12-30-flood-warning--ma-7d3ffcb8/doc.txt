Due to the rainfall experienced in the upper catchment areas of the Mahaweli River over the past few
hours, there is a risk of a flood situation developing within the next 24 hours in the low-lying areas of the
Mahaweli River Basin. This advisory applies specifically to the low-lying areas associated with the
Mahaweli River in the following Divisional Secretariat Divisions: Udunuwara, Udapalatha, Yatinuwara,
Ganga Ihala Korale, Pasbage Korale and Gangawata Korale. Residents living in the low-lying areas in
close proximity to the Mahaweli River within these designated regions are strongly advised to remain
highly vigilant and to take the necessary precautions to ensure their safety against potential flooding.
Furthermore, disaster management authorities are requested to take prompt and appropriate measures
regarding this situation. As further rainfall is anticipated in the period ahead, the general public is
earnestly requested to remain on high alert and exercise the utmost caution.
