Significant, high-intensity rainfall has currently been recorded in the upper and middle catchment areas
of the Kelani River Basin. Based on these rainfall conditions and the analysis of water levels at river
gauges maintained by the Irrigation Department within the basin, a warning is hereby issued that a minor
flood situation could develop within the next 24 hours. This warning applies to the low-lying areas of
the Kelani River Valley located within the following Divisional Secretariat Divisions: Dompe,
Seethawaka, Homagama, Kaduwela, Biyagama and Kolonnawa. Furthermore, according to current
weather forecasts, further high-intensity rainfall is anticipated, which could lead to a rapid and sudden
increase in river water levels. Therefore, residents living in these areas, individuals using the river, and
motorists traveling through these regions are strongly advised to exercise extreme caution and remain
highly vigilant regarding this developing situation.Disaster management authorities are requested to take
the necessary proactive measures in this regard.
