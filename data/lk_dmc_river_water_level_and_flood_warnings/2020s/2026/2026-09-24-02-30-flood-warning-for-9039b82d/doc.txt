Managing Rain Water --- Make Sri Lanka Prosperous

Significant heavy rainfall has been received over the catchment areas of the Gin Ganga River Basin
to date, and rainfall is continuing. Based on the prevailing rainfall conditions and an analysis of the
water levels recorded at the river gauging stations maintained by the Irrigation Department within the
Gin Ganga River Basin, a Minor Flood Situation is likely to occur within the next 24 hours from
now in low-lying areas of the Gin Ganga valley within the Thawalama, Nagoda, Baddegama and
Welivitiya-Divithura Divisional Secretariat Divisions. The general public residing in these areas
and vehicle drivers travelling through the affected areas are hereby requested to exercise extreme
caution in view of this situation. The relevant Disaster Management Authorities are requested to
take necessary precautionary measures in this regard.
