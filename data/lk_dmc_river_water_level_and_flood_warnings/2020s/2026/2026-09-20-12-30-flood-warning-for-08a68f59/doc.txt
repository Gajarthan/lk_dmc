Secretariat Divisions of Thawalama, Nagoda, Baddegama and Welivitiya-Divithura during the
next 24 hours. Residents living in the above-mentioned areas, as well as motorists travelling through
these areas, are urged to exercise extreme caution and remain highly vigilant regarding the prevailing
situation. The relevant disaster management authorities are requested to take all necessary
measures in response to this situation.
