Managing Rain Water --- Make Sri Lanka Prosperous

Based on the heavy rainfall currently being experienced over the Nilwala River Basin, together with an
analysis of the water levels recorded at river gauging stations maintained by the Irrigation Department
within the Nilwala River Basin, a Minor Flood Situation is likely to occur within the next 24 hours in
low-lying areas of the Nilwala River floodplain within the Pasgoda, Kotapola, Pitabeddara, Akuressa,
Athuraliya, Malimbada, Kamburupitiya and Thihagoda Divisional Secretariat Divisions. The
general public residing in these areas and vehicle drivers travelling through the affected areas are hereby
requested to exercise extreme caution in view of this situation. The relevant Disaster Management
Authorities are requested to take necessary precautionary measures in this regard.
