Due to the rainfall received in the upper catchment areas of the Kuda Ganga sub-basin of the Kalu Ganga
and the fact that the spill gates of the Kukule Ganga reservoir have been opened by now, according to
the analysis of river water levels maintained by the Irrigation Department in the Kalu Ganga basin, a
Minor Flood Situation may occur within the next 48 hours in the low-lying areas belonging to the
floodplains of the Kuda Ganga and Maguru Ganga in the Bulathsinhala, Madurawala, and
Palindanuwara Divisional Secretariat Divisions. Consequently, there is a risk of by-roads running
through the low-lying areas around the Kuda Ganga and Maguru Ganga becoming inundated. The public
living in these areas and motorists traveling through them are requested to act with caution
regarding this situation. Furthermore, disaster management authorities are requested to take the
necessary actions in this regard.
