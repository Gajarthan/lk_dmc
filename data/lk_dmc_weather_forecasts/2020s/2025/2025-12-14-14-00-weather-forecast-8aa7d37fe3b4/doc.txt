WEATHER FORECAST FOR 15 DECEMBER 2025
Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 14 December 2025
Under the influence of an Easterly wave, showery conditions are expected to enhance to some
extent over the island from December 16th.
Several spells of showers will occur in Northern, North-Central, Eastern, Uva, Central and
Northwestern provinces.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at several places in the other areas of the island after 1.00
p.m.
Fairly strong winds of about (30-40) kmph can be expected at times over Eastern slopes of the
central hills, Northern, North-central and North-western provinces and in Trincomalee,
Hambantota and Monaragala districts.
Misty conditions can be expected at some places in Sabaragamuwa, Central provinces and in Galle
and Matara districts during the early hours of the morning.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damages caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
Rainfall amount (mm): Light: 00-12.5, Light to moderate: 12.5-25, Moderate: 25-50, Fairly heavy rain: 50 -100,
Heavy rain: 100 – 150, Very heavy rain: 150 or more.
