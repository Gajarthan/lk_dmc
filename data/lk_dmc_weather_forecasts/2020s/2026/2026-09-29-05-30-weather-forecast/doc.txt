WEATHER FORECAST FOR 29 SEPTEMBER 2026
Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 29 September 2026.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at several places in Uva, Eastern and Central provinces and
in Polonnaruwa and Mullaittivu districts after 2.00 p.m. Fairly heavy falls above 50 mm are likely
at some places in the above areas.
A Few shower may occur in the coastal areas of the Western Province and the Galle and Matara
districts during the morning.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Heavy rain: 100 – 150, Very heavy rain: 150 or more.
