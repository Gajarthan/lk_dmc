Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.30 a.m. 15 August 2026
Valid for the period until 10.30 a.m. 16 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Matara to Pottuvil via
Hambantota and from, Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via Mannar and
Puttalam.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts
extending from Matara to Pottuvil
via Hambantota and from,
Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via
Mannar and Puttalam will be rough
at times with strong gusty winds up
to (55-60) kmph.
Wind speed can increase up to (45-
50) kmph and may be fairly rough at
times in the other sea areas around
the island.
The naval and fishing communities
are requested to be vigilant in this
regard.
