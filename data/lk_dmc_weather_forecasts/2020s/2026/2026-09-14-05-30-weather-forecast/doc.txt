Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 14 September 2026.
Due to a low-level atmospheric disturbance in the vicinity of Sri Lanka, cloudy skies are expected
over the Western, Central, Sabaragamuwa, Southern and Uva provinces, with showers or
thundershowers occurring at times.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in other parts of the Island after 1.00 p.m.
Heavy falls above 100 mm are likely at some places in Western, Central, Sabaragamuwa, Southern,
North-central and North-western provinces.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Heavy rain: 100 – 150, Very heavy rain: 150 or more.
