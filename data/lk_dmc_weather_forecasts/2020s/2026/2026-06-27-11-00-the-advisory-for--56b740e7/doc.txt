COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 11.00 a.m. 27 June 2026
Valid for the period until 11.00 a.m. 28 June 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kalpitiya to Kankasanthurai via
Mannar and from Hambantota to Pottuvil
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Kalpitiya to Kankasanthurai via
Mannar and from Hambantota to
Pottuvil will be rough at times with
strong gusty winds up to (50-60)
kmph.
Naval and fishing communities are
requested to be vigilant and be
attentive to future forecasts issued by
the Department of Meteorology in
this regard.
