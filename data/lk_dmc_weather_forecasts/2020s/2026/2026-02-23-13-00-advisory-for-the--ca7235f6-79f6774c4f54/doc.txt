Advisory for the low pressure area over Southwest Bay of Bengal.
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre, Department of Meteorology.
Issued at 1.00 p.m. on 23 January 2026
For fishermen and naval communities engaged in marine activities in
the Bay of Bengal, northeast of Sri Lanka.
PLEASE BE AWARE!
The low-pressure area over the Bay of Bengal, to the northeast of Sri Lanka, is moving
northeastwards and is expected to weaken further during the next 24 hours. Under the
influence of this system, heavy showers or thundershowers, Strong wind gust about (50-60)
kmph, may occur in the sea areas marked on the map below, and the mentioned sea areas may
be rough.
Navel and fishing communities are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and
bulletins issued by the Department of Meteorology in this regard.
