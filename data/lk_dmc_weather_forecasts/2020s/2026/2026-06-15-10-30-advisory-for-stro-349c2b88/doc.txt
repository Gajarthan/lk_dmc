Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.30 a.m. 15 June 2026
Valid for the period until 10.30 a.m. 16 June 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota
will be rough at times with strong gusty
winds up to (50-60) kmph. Naval and
fishing communities are requested to be
vigilant in this regard.
The wave height may increase about (2.0
– 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast
extending from Kalpitiya to Pottuvil via
Colombo, Galle and Hambantota.
Naval and fishing communities are
requested to be attentive to future
forecasts issued by the Department of
Meteorology in this regard.
