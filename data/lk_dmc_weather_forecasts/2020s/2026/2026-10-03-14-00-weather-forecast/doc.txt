WEATHER FORECAST FOR 04 October 2026
Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 03 October 2026
Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in most part of the island after 1.00 p.m.
Fairly heavy falls above 75 mm are likely at some places in Central, Sabaragamuwa, Western,
Southern, Uva, North-western and North-central provinces.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
Misty conditions can be expected at some places in Central, Sabaragamuwa and Uva provinces
during the early hours of the morning.
Rainfall amount (mm): Light: 00-12.5, Light to moderate: 12.5-25, Moderate: 25-50, Fairly heavy rain: 50 -100,
Heavy rain: 100 – 150, Very heavy rain: 150 or more.
