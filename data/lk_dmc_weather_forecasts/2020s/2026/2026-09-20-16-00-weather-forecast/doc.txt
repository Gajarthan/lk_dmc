GENERAL WEATHER FORECAST FOR NEXT 36 HOURS
Issued at 04.00 p.m. on 20 September 2026
A low-pressure area has formed over the Bay of Bengal, to the northeast of the island, in the
vicinity of the Andaman region. Although this system will not have a direct impact on Sri Lanka,
the prevailing rainy conditions in the southwestern part of the island are expected to continue
due to the wind flow towards the system.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western
provinces and in Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya Districts. Heavy falls above 100 mm are
likely at some places in the above areas.
Several spells of showers may occur in North-central province and Mannar district.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in Uva province and in Batticaloa and
Ampara districts after 2.00 p.m.
Fairly strong winds of about (30-40) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the
Central hills, North-central and North-western provinces and in Trincomalee, Hambantota and
Matale Districts.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused
by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
