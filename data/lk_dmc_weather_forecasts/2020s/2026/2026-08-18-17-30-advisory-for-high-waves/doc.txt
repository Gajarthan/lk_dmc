COLOR: Amber
Advisory for High Waves.
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 05.30 p.m. 18 August 2026
Valid for the period until 05.30 p.m. 19 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Negombo to Pottuvil via Galle,
Matara and Hambantota.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The swell waves (about 2.5 m – 3.0 m)
height (This is not for land area) may
increase in the sea areas off the coast
extending from Negombo to Pottuvil
via Galle, Matara and Hambantota.
There is a possibility that near shore
sea areas off the coast extending from
Beruwala to Matara via Galle, may
experience surges due to swell waves.
The naval and fishing communities who
are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
