GENERAL WEATHER FORECAST FOR NEXT 36 HOURS
Issued at 04.00 p.m. on 05 August 2026
Due to the active southwest monsoon, the prevailing rainy and windy conditions over the
southwestern parts of the island are expected to intensify from tomorrow and continue for the
next few days.
For next 36 hours:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in Western, Sabaragamuwa, and North-western
provinces and Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya, Galle and Matara districts. Cloudy skies will prevail over these
areas.
Heavy falls above 100 mm are likely at some places in Western and Sabaragamuwa provinces and
in Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya and Galle districts.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in Ampara, Batticaloa, and Monaragala
districts after 2.00 p.m.
Strong winds of about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over North-central, Sabaragamuwa,
Southern, Central and North-western provinces.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused
by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
