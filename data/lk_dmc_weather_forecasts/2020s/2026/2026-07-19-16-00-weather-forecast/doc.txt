Several spells of showers will occur in Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western provinces and
in Kandy, Nuwara-Eliya, Galle and Matara districts.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in Ampara and Monaragala districts after

Mainly dry weather will prevail over the other areas of the island.
Strong winds about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of Central hills,
Northern, North-central, Central and North-western provinces and in Rathnapura, Hambantota
and Trincomalee districts.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
