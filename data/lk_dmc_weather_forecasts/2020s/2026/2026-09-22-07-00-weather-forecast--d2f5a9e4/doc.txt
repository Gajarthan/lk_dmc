Weather forecast for multi-day boats
For the sea areas in between 20N - 10S and 60E-100E
(Issued at 07.00 a.m. on 22 September 2026, for next 24 hours)
Sea Areas Around the island
The low-pressure area that was prevailing over the sea area of the Bay of Bengal, to the northeast of
the island, has now developed into a depression. It is expected to further develop into a deep
depression within the next 24 hours.
Due to its influence, wind and rainy condition over the Bay of Bengal will increase. Naval and fishing
communities are warned not to venture to the sea areas in between 5°N and 22°N latitude and 85°E and
100°E longitude until further notice. Naval and fishing communities operating in those sea areas are
advised to move away from the affected sea areas.
Navel and fishing communities engaging the activities over this sea areas are advised to be vigilant and
be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the department of meteorology in this
regards.
Condition of Rain:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai
to Hambanthota via Puttalam, Colombo and Galle. Showers or thundershowers may occur at several places
in the other sea areas during the evening or night.
Winds:
Winds will be South-westerly. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.
Wind speed can increase up to (55-60) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via Mannar, and from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota.
State of Sea:
The sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via Mannar, and from Matara to
Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough at times.
The wave height may increase about (2.0 – 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Matara.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
