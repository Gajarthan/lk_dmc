මස 21 වන දින වන විට නැවගනහිර-මධ්‍යම වබංගාල වබාක්ක ආශ්‍රිතව ක්‍රමවයන්ත පීඩන අවපාතයක් දක්වා

Bulletin No: 02 MSA/26/09/19/01
COLOR: Amber
Advisory for strong winds and rough seas.
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 05.00 p.m. 19 September 2026, valid for the next 24 hours.
For Multi-day boats in the Bay of Bengal deep sea areas.
PLEASE BE AWARE!
A low pressure area is very likely to form over the Andaman sea in the Bay of Bengal today
evening (19th) and It is very likely to move West-Northwestwards and intensify gradually into
a depression over East-Central Bay of Bengal on 21st of September 2026. Due to its influence,
wind speeds over the Bay of Bengal will increase during the next few days starting from today.
The wind speed in the sea areas marked under the “Advisory” category on the map below will
increase to 55-65 kmph at times and those sea areas will be rough or very rough at times.
Navel and fishing communities engaging the activities over this sea area are advised to be
vigilant and be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the department of
meteorology in this regards.
