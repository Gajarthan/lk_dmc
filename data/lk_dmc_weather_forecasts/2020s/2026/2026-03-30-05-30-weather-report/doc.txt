WEATHER FORECAST FOR 30 MARCH 2026
Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 30 March 2026.
Warm weather conditions prevailing over the island are expected to continue.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in Western, Sabaragamuwa and
Northwestern provinces and in Galle, and Matara districts after 2.00 pm.
Mainly dry weather will prevail over the other parts of the island.
Misty conditions can be expected at some places in Central and Sabaragamuwa provinces and
in Galle and Matara districts during the early hours of the morning.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damages
caused by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
Rainfall amount (mm): Light: 00-12.5, Light to moderate: 12.5-25, Moderate: 25-50, Fairly heavy rain: 50 -100,
Heavy rain: 100 – 150, Very heavy rain: 150 or more.
