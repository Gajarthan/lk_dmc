WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS
Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 23 May 2026
Condition of Rain:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Hambantota via Colombo and Galle. Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places
elsewhere around the island in the evening or night.
Winds:
Winds will be southwesterly. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph. Wind speed can increase up to (50-60) kmph
at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and
Hambantota. Wind speed can increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the island.
State of Sea:
The sea areas off the coasts extending from Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Hambantota will
be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island will be fairly rough at times.
The wave height may increase about (2.0 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Hambantota (this is not for land area).
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
