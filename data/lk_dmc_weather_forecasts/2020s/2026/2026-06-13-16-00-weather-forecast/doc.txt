Issued at 04.00 p.m. on 13 June 2026
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western
provinces and in Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts. Fairly heavy falls about 75 mm
are likely at some places in these areas.
Several spells of showers may occur in Mannar and Anuradhapura districts.
Strong winds about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the central
hills, Northern and North-central provinces and in Hambantota and Trincomalee districts. Fairly
strong winds about (30-40) kmph can be expected at times over other areas of island.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused
by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
