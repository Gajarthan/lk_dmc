Special Weather Bulletin.

PLEASE BE AWARE!
The low-level atmospheric disturbance in the Bay of Bengal, to the southeast of the Sri
Lanka is likely to intensify further. Hence, showery condition over the island, particularly in
the Northern, North-Central, Eastern, Uva and Central provinces is expected to enhance
from January 8th.
The general public are requested to be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued
by the Department of Meteorology in this regards.
