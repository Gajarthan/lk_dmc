COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 06.30 p.m. 03 October 2026
Valid for the period until 11.00 p.m. 03 October 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Mannar to Matara via Puttalam,
Colombo and Galle.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
Showers or thundershowers will occur
in the sea areas off the coast extending
from Mannar to Matara via Puttalam,
Colombo and Galle during the evening
or night.
Temporarily strong gusty winds (60-70
kmph) and very rough seas can be
expected during thundershowers.
The naval and fishing communities
who are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
