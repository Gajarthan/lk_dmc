COLOR: Amber
Advisory for High Waves.
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 03.30 p.m. 26 August 2026
Valid for the period until 03.30 p.m. 27 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Colombo to Pottuvil via Galle,
Matara and Hambantota.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The swell waves (about 2.0 m – 3.0 m)
height (This is not for land area) may
increase in the sea areas off the coast
extending from Colombo to Pottuvil via
Galle, Matara and Hambantota.
There is a possibility that near shore
sea areas off the coast extending from
Beruwala to Matara via Galle, may
experience surges due to swell waves.
Naval and fishing communities
engaged in activities in the
aforementioned sea areas, as well as
coastal communities living in the
aforementioned nearshore areas, are
advised to remain vigilant in this regard
