Issued at 05.30 a.m. on 15 March 2026.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central,
North-western and Uva provinces and in Galle, Matara, Mannar and Anuradhapura districts
after 2.00 pm.

and North-western provinces.
Misty conditions can be expected at some places in Central, Sabaragamuwa and Uva provinces
and in Galle and Matara districts during the early hours of the morning.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damages caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
