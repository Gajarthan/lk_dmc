Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly
rough at times.
The wave height may increase about (2.0 – 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Matara.
Naval and fishing communities engaged in activities in the aforementioned sea areas, are advised to
remain vigilant in this regard.
