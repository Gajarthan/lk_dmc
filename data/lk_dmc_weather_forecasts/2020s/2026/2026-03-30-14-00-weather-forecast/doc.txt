WEATHER FORECAST FOR 31 March 2026
Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 30 March 2026
Warm weather conditions prevailing over the island are expected to continue.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in Western and Sabaragamuwa provinces
and in Galle and Matara districts after 2.00 pm.
Mainly dry weather will prevail over the other parts of the island.
Misty conditions can be expected at some places in Central, Sabaragamuwa, North-central and
Uva provinces and in Galle and Matara districts during the early hours of the morning.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damages caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
