COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.30 a.m. 30 August 2026
Valid for the period until 10.30 a.m. 31 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kankasanthurai to Puttalam via
Mannar andfrom Hambantota to Pottuvil.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Kankasanthurai to Puttalam via
Mannar and from Hambantota to
Pottuvil will be rough at times with
strong gusty winds up to (50-60)
kmph.
The wave height may increase about
(2.0 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off
the coast extending from Negombo to
Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and
Matara. There is a possibility of
seawater inundation over the near
shore areas extending from Beruwala
to Matara via Galle.
Naval and fishing communities
engaged in activities in the
aforementioned sea areas, as well as
coastal communities living in the
aforementioned nearshore areas, are
advised to remain vigilant in this
regard.
