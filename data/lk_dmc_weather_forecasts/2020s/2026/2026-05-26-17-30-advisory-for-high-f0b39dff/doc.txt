Bulletin No: 01 MHA/2026/05/26/01
COLOR: Amber
Advisory for High Waves and Strong Winds.
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 04.30 p.m. 26 May 2026
For the period from 04.30 p.m. 26 May 2026 to 04.30 p.m. 27 May 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kalpitiya to Pottuvil via Colombo,
Galle and Hambantota.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas extending from Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via Mannar and from Matara to
Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough at times with strong gusty winds up to 60 kmph.
The swell waves (about 2.0 m – 3.0 m) height (This is not for land area) and wave period
(about 10s-14s) may increase in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kalpitiya to
Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle, and Hambantota.
There is a possibility that near shore sea areas off the coast extending from Kalpitiya to
Matara via Colombo and Galle, may experience surges due to swell waves.
The sea areas off the coast extending from Kalpitiya to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and
Hambantota can be rough at times.
The naval and fishing communities who are engaged in fishing and naval activities in the
above sea areas are requested to be vigilant in this regard.
