Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 02.00 p.m. 24 September 2026
Valid for the period until 02.00 p.m. 25 September 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Trincomalee to Negombo via
Kankasanthurai and Mannar, and from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Trincomalee to Negombo via
Kankasanthurai and Mannar, and from
Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota will
be very rough at times with strong
gusty winds up to (55-65) kmph.
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Negombo to Galle via Beruwala
will be rough at times with strong
gusty winds up to (50-55) kmph.
The wave height may increase about
(2.0 – 2.5) meters in the sea areas off
the coast extending from Puttalam to
Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and
Matara.
The naval and fishing communities
who are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
