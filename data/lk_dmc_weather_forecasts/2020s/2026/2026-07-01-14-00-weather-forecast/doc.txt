Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 30 July 2026
Condition of Rain:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at a few places in the sea areas off the coast extending from Chilaw
to Matara via Colombo and Galle.
Winds:
Winds will be south-westerly. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph. Wind speed can increase up to (50-60) kmph
at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Kankasanthurai to Kalpitiya via Mannar and from,
Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota. Wind speed can increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas
around the island.
State of Sea:
The sea areas off the coasts extending from Kankasanthurai to Kalpitiya via Mannar and from, Matara to
Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough at times. The other sea areas around the island may be fairly rough
at times.
The wave height may increase about (2.0 – 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from Chilaw
to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Hambantota.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
