Valid for the period until 09.00 a.m. 27 September 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Chilaw to Pottuvil via Colombo,
Galle and Matara.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The wave height may increase about
(2.0 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off
the coast extending from Chilaw to
Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and
Matara.
There is a possibility of seawater
inundation over the near shore areas
extending from Beruwala to Matara
via Galle.
Naval and fishing communities
engaged in activities in the
aforementioned sea areas, as well as
coastal communities living in the
aforementioned nearshore areas, are
advised to remain vigilant in this
regard.
