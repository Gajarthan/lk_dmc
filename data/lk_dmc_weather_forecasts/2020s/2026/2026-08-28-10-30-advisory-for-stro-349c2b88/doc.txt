Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre

Valid for the period until 10.30 a.m. 29 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kankasanthurai to Puttalam via
Mannar, Mullaitivu to Trincomalee andfrom Hambantota to
Pottuvil.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Kankasanthurai to Puttalam via
Mannar, Mullaitivu to Trincomalee
and from Hambantota to Pottuvil will
be rough at times with strong gusty
winds up to (50-60) kmph.
The wave height may increase about
(2.0 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off
the coast extending from Puttalam to
Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and
Matara.
The naval and fishing communities are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
