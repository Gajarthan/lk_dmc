Bulletin No: 04 MWA/2026/05/31/01
COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 04.30 p.m. 31 May 2026
Valid for the period until 04.30 p.m. 01 June 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Mullaittivu to Chilaw via
Kankasanthurai, Mannar and from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas extending from
Mullaittivu to Chilaw via
Kankasanthurai and Mannar, and from
Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota will
be very rough at times with strong
gusty winds up to (60-65) kmph.
The naval and fishing communities who
are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
