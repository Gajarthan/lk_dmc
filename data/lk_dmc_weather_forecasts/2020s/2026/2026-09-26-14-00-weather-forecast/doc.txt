Wind speed can increase up to (50-60) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from

Chilaw to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Matara.

Matara via Galle.
Naval and fishing communities engaged in activities in the aforementioned sea areas, as well as
coastal communities living in the aforementioned nearshore areas, are advised to remain vigilant in
this regard.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
