COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.30 a.m. 14 June 2026
Valid for the period until 10.30 a.m. 15 June 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kalpitiya to Pottuvil via Colombo,
Galle and Hambantota
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Kalpitiya to Pottuvil via Colombo
Galle and Hambantota will be rough at
times with strong gusty winds up to (50-
60) kmph. Naval and fishing communities
are requested to be vigilant in this
regard.
The warning issued earlier advising naval
and fishing communities not to venture
into the sea areas off the coast extending
from Kankasanthurai to Kalpitiya via
Mannar, is hereby withdrawn.
The wave height may increase about (2.0
– 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast
extending from Mannar to Pottuvil via
Colombo, Galle and Hambantota.
Naval and fishing communities are requested to be attentive to future forecasts issued by
the Department of Meteorology in this regard.
