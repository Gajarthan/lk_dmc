Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 04.30 p.m. 28 May 2026
For the period from 04.30 p.m. 28 May 2026 to 04.30 p.m. 29 May 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via
Mannar and from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas extending from Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via Mannar and from Galle to
Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough at times with strong gusty winds up to (60-65) kmph.
The naval and fishing communities who are engaged in fishing and naval activities in the
above sea areas are requested to be vigilant in this regard.
