COLOR: Red
Warning for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 7.00 a.m. 10 August 2026
Valid for the period until 7.00 a.m. 11 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Matara to Pottuvil via
Hambantota
TAKE ACTION!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota
will be very rough at times with strong
gusty winds exceed 60 kmph. Naval and
fishing communities are advised not to
venture into these sea areas, until
further notice.
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Batticaloa to Chilaw via
Trincomalee, Kankasanthurai and
Mannar, and from Galle to Matara will
be rough at times with strong gusty
winds up to 50 kmph. The naval and
fishing communities who are engaged in
fishing and naval activities in the above
sea areas are requested to be vigilant in
this regard.
Temporarily strong gusty winds also and
very rough seas can be expected during
thundershowers.
Naval and fishing communities are requested to be attentive to future forecasts issued by
the Department of Meteorology in this regard.
