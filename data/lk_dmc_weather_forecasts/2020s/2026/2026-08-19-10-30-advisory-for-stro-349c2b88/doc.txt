Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.30 a.m. 19 August 2026
Valid for the period until 10.30 a.m. 20 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kankasanthurai to Chilaw via
Mannarand from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts
extending from Kankasanthurai to
Chilaw via Mannar, and from Matara
to Pottuvil via Hambantota will be
rough at times with strong gusty
winds up to (50-60) kmph.
The wave height may increase about
(2.5 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off
the coast extending from Negombo
to Pottuvil via Galle and Matara.
These near shore areas may
experience seawater inundation as
well.
The naval and fishing communities
are requested to be vigilant in this
regard.
