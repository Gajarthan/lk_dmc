Bulletin No: 09 LWA/26/06/11/01
COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Strong winds
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 06.00 p.m. 11 June 2026, valid until 06.00 p.m. 12 June 2026
For Central, Sabaragamuwa, Northern, North-central provinces and
Trincomalee, Hambantota districts.
BE PREPARED!
Strong winds of about (40-50)
kmph can be expected at times
over Central, Sabaragamuwa,
Northern and North-central
provinces and in Trincomalee and
Hambantota districts, due to the
active south-west monsoonal
condition.
Fairly strong winds about (30-40)
kmph can be expected at times
elsewhere of the island.
Color: -Red Color: -Amber Color: -Green
Waning (Take action) Advisory (Be prepared) Threat is over

DAMAGE EXPECTED:
• Damage to roof tiles/ sheets etc.
• Damage to power and communication lines.
• Breaking of tree branches and uprooting of large avenue trees.
• Damage to paddy crops, banana, papaya trees and orchards.
• Damage to harbor yachts
• Sea water inundation in low lying areas in the near coast.
ACTION SUGGESTED:
• Be cautious of potential hazards caused by damaged roof tiles or sheets.
• Beware of fallen trees and power lines.
• Be vigilant regarding impending extreme weather situation.
• For emergency assistance contact the local disaster management authorities.
• Requested to be attentive about future advisories issued by the Department of Meteorology
in this regard.
