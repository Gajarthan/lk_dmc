Bulletin No: 05 MSW/26/09/21/02
COLOR: Red
Warning for strong winds and rough seas.
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.00 a.m. 21 September 2026, valid for the next 24 hours.
For Multi-day boats in the Bay of Bengal deep sea areas.
PLEASE BE AWARE!
The Low Pressure area over East-central Bay of Bengal, is very likely to move West-
northwestwards and intensify gradually into a depression over East-central Bay of Bengal on
21st and into a deep depression over West-central Bay of Bengal on 22nd September.
Continuing to move west-northwestwards it is very likely to reach West-central and adjoining
northwest Bay of Bengal near north Andhra Pradesh - South Odisha coasts around 0000 UTC
of 23rd September.
The wind speed in the sea areas marked under the “Advisory” category on the map below will
increase to (55-65) kmph at times and those sea areas will be rough or very rough at times.
Wind speeds in the sea areas marked under the “Warning” category may increase to 70kmph
at times, heavy thundershowers can occur, and the sea conditions in these areas will become
very rough.
Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the sea areas in between 5°N and
22°N latitude and 85°E and 100°E longitude until further notice. Naval and fishing communities
operating in those sea areas are advised to move away from the affected sea areas.
Navel and fishing communities engaging the activities over this sea area are advised to be
vigilant and be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the department of
meteorology in this regards.
