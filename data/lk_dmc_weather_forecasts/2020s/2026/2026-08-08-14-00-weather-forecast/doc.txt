The sea areas off the coasts extending from Batticaloa to Mannar via Trincomalee and
Kankasanthurai and from Chilaw to Matara via Colombo and Galle will be rough at times. The naval
and fishing communities who are engaged in fishing and naval activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
The wave height may increase about (2.5 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Hambantota.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
