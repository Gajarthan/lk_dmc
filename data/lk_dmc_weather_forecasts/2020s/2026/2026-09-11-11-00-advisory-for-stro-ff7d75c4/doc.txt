Bulletin No: 04 MWA/26/09/11/01
COLOR: Amber
Advisory for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 11.00 a.m. 11 September 2026
Valid for the period until 11.00 a.m. 12 September 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Hambantota to Pottuvil.
PLEASE BE PREPARED!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Hambantota to Pottuvil will be
rough at times with strong gusty
winds up to (50-60) kmph.
The wave height may increase about
(2.0 – 3.0) meters in the sea areas off
the coast extending from Chilaw to
Pottuvil via Galle, Matara and
Hambantota.
There is a possibility of seawater
inundation over the near shore areas
extending from Beruwala to Matara
via Galle.
The naval and fishing communities
who are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
