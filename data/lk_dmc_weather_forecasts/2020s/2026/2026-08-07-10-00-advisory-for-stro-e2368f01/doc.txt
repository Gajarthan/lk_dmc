Warning for Strong Winds and Rough Seas
Issued by the Natural Hazards Early Warning Centre
Issued at 10.00 a.m. 07 August 2026
Valid for the period until 10.00 a.m. 08 August 2026.
For the sea areas extending from Kalpitiya to Colombo and from
Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota
TAKE ACTION!
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Kalpitiya to Colombo and from
Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota will
be very rough at times with strong
gusty winds up to (60-70) kmph. Naval
and fishing communities are advised
not to venture into these sea areas,
until further notice.
The sea areas off the coasts extending
from Batticaloa to Kalpitiya via
Trincomalee, Kankasanthurai and
Mannar and from Colombo to Matara
via Galle will be rough at times with
strong gusty winds up to (50-60) kmph.
The naval and fishing communities
who are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard.
Naval and fishing communities are
requested to be attentive to future
forecasts issued by the Department of
Meteorology in this regard.
