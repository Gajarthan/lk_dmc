Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 19 September 2026
Showery weather in the Southwestern parts of the island is likely to increase during the next
few days starting from today (19th).
Showers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western provinces and in
Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya Districts. Heavy falls about 100 mm are likely at some
places in western and Sabaragamuwa provinces and in Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at several places in Uva and Northern provinces and in
Batticaloa and Ampara districts after 2.00 p.m.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused
by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

ජාතික කාලගුණ විදයා මධ්‍යස්ථානහේ, අනාවැකි අංශය මගින් නිකුත් කරන ලදි
Issued by Forecasting Division, National Meteorological Centre
WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS
Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 19 September 2026
A low pressure area is very likely to form over the Andaman sea in the Bay of Bengal today (19th)
which is likely to get intensified in next few days.
Due to its influence, wind speeds over the Bay of Bengal will increase during the next few days
starting from today. Navel and fishing communities engaging the activities over this sea areas are
advised to be vigilant and be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the
department of meteorology in this regards.
Condition of Rain:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from Negombo
to Hambanthota via Colombo and Galle. Showers or thundershowers may occur at several places in
the other sea areas during the evening or night.
Winds:
Winds will be South-westerly. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.
Wind speed can increase up to (50-60) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota. Wind speed can increase up to 50 kmph at times in the sea areas
off the coast extending from Puttalam to Kankasanthurai via Mannar.
State of Sea:
The sea areas off the coast extending from Matara to Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough at times.
The sea areas off the coasts extending from Puttalam to Kankasanthurai via Mannar will be fairly
rough at times.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
The wave height may increase about (2.0 – 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Matara.
Rainfall amount (mm): Light: 00-12.5, Light to moderate: 12.5-25, Moderate: 25-50, Fairly heavy rain: 50 -100,
Heavy rain: 100 – 150, Very heavy rain: 150 or more.
