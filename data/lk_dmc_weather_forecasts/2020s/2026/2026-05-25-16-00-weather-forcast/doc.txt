Issued at 04.00 p.m. on 25 May 2026.
Southwest Monsoon is gradually establishing across the island, an increase in rainfall over the
southwestern parts of the island is expected during the next few days.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in Western, Sabaragamuwa and North-western
provinces and in Galle, Matara, Kandy and Nuwara-Eliya districts. Heavy falls about 100 mm are
likely at some places in these areas.
Showers or thundershowers may occur at a few places in Uva province and in Ampara and
Batticaloa district after 1.00 pm.
Fairly strong winds about (30-40) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the
central hills, Northern, North-central, North-western and Southern provinces and in Trincomalee
district.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused
by temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.
