TAKE ACTION!

from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota
will be very rough at times with very
strong gusty winds up to (60-70)
kmph. Naval and fishing communities
are advised not to venture into these
sea areas, until further notice.

from Puttalam to Galle via Colombo
will be rough at times with strong
gusty winds up to (50-60) kmph.
The naval and fishing communities
who are engaged in fishing and naval
activities in the above sea areas are
requested to be vigilant in this regard
and be attentive to future forecasts
issued by the Department of
Meteorology in this regard.
