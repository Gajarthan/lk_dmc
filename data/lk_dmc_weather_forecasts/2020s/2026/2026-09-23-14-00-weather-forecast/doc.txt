Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 23 September 2026
The Deep Depression over the west-central and adjoining northwest Bay of Bengal is very
likely to move west-northwestwards and cross the coastal areas of northern Andhra Pradesh
and southern Odisha, India, during tonight. Although this system will not have a direct impact
on Sri Lanka, the prevailing rainy conditions in the southwestern part of the island are
expected to continue due to the wind flow towards the system.
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the Western, Sabaragamuwa, Central and
North-western provinces and in Galle and Matara Districts. Heavy falls above 100 mm are likely
at some places in the above areas.
A few showers will occur in North-central province and in Mannar district.
Strong winds of about (40-50) kmph can be expected at times over Western slopes of the Central
hills, North-central and North-western provinces and in Trincomalee, Hambantota and Matale
Districts.
The general public is kindly requested to take adequate precautions to minimize damage caused by
temporary localized strong winds and lightning during thundershowers.

Issued by Forecasting Division, National Meteorological Centre
WEATHER FORECAST FOR SEA AREAS AROUND THE ISLAND DURING NEXT 24 HOURS
Issued at 2.00 p.m. on 23 September 2026
The Deep Depression over the west-central and adjoining northwest Bay of Bengal is currently
located around 1,000 km northeast of Kankasanthurai. The system is very likely to move further west-
northwestwards and cross the coastal areas of northern Andhra Pradesh and southern Odisha, India.
Under the influence of this system, heavy thundershowers are expected over the sea areas of the Bay
of Bengal, with winds occasionally increasing up to (70-80) kmph, and the sea conditions in these
areas will become very rough.
Naval and fishing communities are warned not to venture to the sea areas in between 7°N and 22°N
latitude and 83°E and 95°E longitude until further notice. Naval and fishing communities operating in
those sea areas are advised to move away from the affected sea areas.
Navel and fishing communities engaging the activities over this sea areas are advised to be vigilant
and be attentive to the future forecasts and bulletins issued by the department of meteorology in
this regards.
Condition of Rain:
Showers or thundershowers will occur at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Kankasanthurai to Hambanthota via Puttalam, Colombo and Galle. Showers or thundershowers may
occur at several places in the other sea areas during the evening or night.
Winds:
Winds will be South-westerly. Wind speed will be (30-40) kmph.
Wind speed can increase up to (55-65) kmph at times in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Trincomalee to Negombo via Kankasanthurai and Mannar and Puttalam, and from Galle to Pottuvil
via Hambantota. Wind speed can increase up to 50 kmph at times in the other sea areas around the
island
State of Sea:
The sea areas off the coast extending from Trincomalee to Negombo via Kankasanthurai and Mannar
and Puttalam, and from Galle to Pottuvil via Hambantota will be rough or very rough at times. The
other sea areas around the island will be fairly rough at times
The wave height may increase about (2.0 – 2.5) meters in the sea areas off the coast extending from
Puttalam to Pottuvil via Colombo, Galle and Matara.
Temporarily strong gusty winds and very rough seas can be expected during thundershowers.
